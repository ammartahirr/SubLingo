Player

🎬 While watching (Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Hulu, any HTML5 player)

  1. Turn on subtitles in the player.
  2. Press Ctrl + Q. The video pauses and pick mode starts.
  3. Click any word in the subtitle on screen. You can also click a word in the copy of the subtitle line shown above the hint bar.
  4. A panel opens with the meaning in the scene, the dictionary meanings, pronunciation (🔊 normal / 🐢 slow), when to use it, its meaning in different sentences and 10 example sentences.
  5. Click more words, or click words inside the example sentences to look those up too. Press ☆ to save a word.
  6. Press Ctrl + Q again (or Esc) to close it and resume the video.
